# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance / Test Lead defines and validates the quality bar for the delivery team. They ensure work meets acceptance criteria, release readiness, and user expectations before changes are shipped.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage goals, and acceptance validation approach
- Review critical user journeys and regression risk areas
- Coordinate test planning across unit, integration, and smoke test layers
- Verify that delivered work meets the Definition of Done
- Escalate release blockers or quality risks to PM and product leadership

### Goals
- Reduce defect escape and release risk
- Improve confidence in handoffs across planning, build, and deployment
- Support measurable quality outcomes for users and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- QA planning and release readiness reviews
- QA sign-off in sprint or milestone review meetings
- Coordination with developers, PMs, and release stakeholders on blockers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with developers on validation, testing strategy, and triage
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and customer-facing quality expectations
- Reports release risks or readiness gaps to the Project Manager for timeline and communication decisions

---

## UX / Research Partner

### Role Summary
The UX / Research Partner ensures user needs, usability, and experience quality remain central to product decisions and delivery work.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer and user insights through interviews, usability testing, and feedback review
- Translate research findings into user flows, service patterns, and experience goals
- Collaborate on design quality, accessibility, and usability standards
- Help prioritize experience improvements against delivery trade-offs
- Ensure product decisions are informed by evidence and user impact

### Goals
- Improve user experience and adoption outcomes
- Bring customer perspectives into planning and delivery decisions
- Reduce rework caused by weak problem framing or unclear experience requirements

### Typical Communication
- Early discovery and planning workshops
- Design critiques, customer feedback review, and usability test sessions
- Participation in roadmap and prioritization discussions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to validate user problems and success metrics
- Supports developers by clarifying behavior, flows, and edge cases
- Keeps PMs and stakeholders informed of usability risks and opportunities

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Lead helps the team identify, assess, and reduce security, privacy, and regulatory risks in project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, dependencies, and workflows for security and compliance impacts
- Support secure design principles, privacy considerations, and policy compliance
- Coordinate security testing, vulnerability review, and remediation planning
- Ensure required controls, documentation, and approvals are in place for delivery and release
- Escalate high-risk issues to the appropriate leadership or security channels

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure delivery decisions are aligned with regulatory and organizational standards
- Improve trust and resilience in production systems

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews
- Security triage and remediation planning sessions
- Compliance checkpoints tied to milestones or release approvals

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and decision timing
- Partners with developers during design and implementation to reduce issues before release
- Provides risk updates that inform stakeholder communication and release documentation

---

## Release Manager / Enablement Lead

### Role Summary
The Release Manager / Enablement Lead coordinates release readiness, communication, and operational support so changes are delivered smoothly and safely.

### Responsibilities
- Align deployment plans, schedules, and release readiness checklists
- Coordinate communications for go-live, rollback plans, and stakeholder updates
- Support post-release verification and issue triage across launch activities
- Ensure operational readiness for support and enablement teams
- Maintain a clear handoff between delivery and operations

### Goals
- Reduce launch risk and operational disruption
- Improve consistency in deployment and stakeholder communication
- Support faster adoption and issue resolution during rollout

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch check-ins
- Stakeholder notifications and post-release follow-ups
- Coordination with support, operations, and project leadership

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with PMs to align release timing and stakeholder expectations
- Collaborates with QA and developers to confirm readiness and rollback scenarios
- Supports Product Managers in communicating launch outcomes and follow-up actions

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
The Customer Success / Support Liaison translates customer feedback, support patterns, and service realities into product and delivery priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer pain points, escalation trends, and support issues
- Share post-release feedback into backlog prioritization and improvement planning
- Coordinate with support and operations teams on user impact and communication needs
- Help identify what success looks like from a customer experience perspective
- Support adoption, onboarding, and smooth transition to new capabilities

### Goals
- Align delivery work with real customer needs and service realities
- Reduce friction between product teams and support operations
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption after launch

### Typical Communication
- Weekly customer feedback reviews and service health summaries
- Escalation updates and support coordination notes
- Product and roadmap discussions with PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with evidence from user experience, adoption, and support feedback
- Helps Project Managers and release leads identify operational readiness concerns
- Supports developers by clarifying real-world usage patterns and edge cases

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Lead helps the team define and measure outcomes so decisions are informed by evidence rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation needed to measure success and product health
- Support experiment design, baseline measurement, and performance tracking
- Monitor quality, adoption, usage, and operational signals after release
- Partner with PMs and stakeholders on what data is needed to make decisions
- Help connect delivery work to business and customer outcomes

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through clear metrics and evidence
- Reduce uncertainty in roadmap and prioritization decisions
- Support continuous learning and iteration after launch

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews, experiment planning, and measurement checkpoints
- Stakeholder reporting and milestone updates
- Data quality or instrumentation issue triage

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to define success metrics and evaluation criteria
- Collaborates with developers to ensure analytics and observability are built into features
- Shares data findings that inform PM decisions and release communication
