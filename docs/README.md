# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the project management playbooks and process guidance used by OctoAcme. The goal of these documents is to help the team align on how work is initiated, planned, executed, released, and improved over time.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, cross-functional project lifecycle that begins with initiation and moves through planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement. In the initiation phase, the team confirms the business problem, stakeholders, success metrics, and rough timeline before approving the work.

During planning, work is broken into a prioritized backlog with clear owners, estimated scope, dependencies, and milestones. The team explicitly identifies who is responsible for decisions, quality checks, communications, and release readiness so there are fewer gaps during delivery.

During execution, OctoAcme emphasizes clear ownership, regular communication, and quality gates. The team works in a rhythm of standups, syncs, reviews, and risk tracking, with project managers coordinating schedules and dependencies while product and delivery leads make priority and quality decisions.

Quality assurance is built into the workflow, not treated as a final step. Teams are expected to define clear acceptance criteria, use CI to check tests and security scanning, require code review and sign-off where relevant, and validate with QA and stakeholder feedback before release.

At release time, OctoAcme validates readiness with deployment checks, smoke tests, release notes, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification. After each sprint, milestone, or incident, the team captures learnings and converts them into improvements that keep the process effective and sustainable.

## How to Use This Knowledge Base

Use these documents as the standard process foundation for OctoAcme projects. Keep the project charter, status updates, backlog, release artifacts, and role accountability checklist in the repo or project board so that the team can work from the same source of truth.

When a project is complex or cross-functional, use the persona guide and accountability checklist to clarify responsibilities, dependencies, and escalation paths before work starts.
