# OctoAcme — Role Accountability Checklist

## Purpose
Use this checklist to make ownership explicit across planning, execution, release, and support. It helps reduce ambiguity across cross-functional teams and ensures key responsibilities are not forgotten during handoffs.

## Core Ownership Checklist

### Planning and Scope
- [ ] Problem statement and success metrics are documented
- [ ] Scope, milestones, and dependencies are identified
- [ ] Roles and decision owners are assigned
- [ ] Risks and open questions are captured in the risk register
- [ ] Definition of Done is agreed with product and QA

### Execution and Delivery
- [ ] Work items have clear owners and due dates
- [ ] Dependencies are tracked and escalated as needed
- [ ] Communication plan for stakeholders is active
- [ ] QA sign-off requirements are known before release
- [ ] Security or compliance review is scheduled when applicable
- [ ] UX and customer feedback are considered before final acceptance

### Release and Launch
- [ ] Release plan and communication are agreed
- [ ] Deployment checklist is completed
- [ ] Smoke tests and validation are completed
- [ ] Rollback plan is documented
- [ ] Support / customer success are notified of launch timing and expectations

### Follow-up and Continuous Improvement
- [ ] Retrospective or post-release review is scheduled
- [ ] Action items are assigned with due dates
- [ ] Metrics and usage signals are reviewed after release
- [ ] Outstanding risks are re-checked and updated

## Escalation Path
- Team-level blocker: team lead or project owner
- Cross-team dependency: Project Manager and affected leads
- Product or scope decision: Product Manager and sponsor
- Security or compliance concern: Security / Compliance Lead and leadership
- Release risk or launch issue: Release Manager and Project Manager

## Notes
Use this checklist at kickoff, before milestones, before release, and during retrospectives. It is meant to be lightweight but explicit so accountability is visible to everyone.
